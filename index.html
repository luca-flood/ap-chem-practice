Solubility guidelines
  • Rules 1–2: Group IA and NH₄⁺ salts dissolve.
  • Rule 3: NO₃⁻, ClO₃⁻, ClO₄⁻ and C₂H₃O₂⁻ salts dissolve.
  • Rule 4: Cl⁻, Br⁻ and I⁻ salts usually dissolve; exceptions use Ag⁺, Hg₂²⁺, Hg²⁺ or Pb²⁺. Fluoride is not covered.
  • Rule 5: Sulfates usually dissolve. Ba²⁺, Hg₂²⁺, Hg²⁺, Pb²⁺ and Sr²⁺ are insoluble exceptions; CaSO₄ and Ag₂SO₄ are slightly soluble.
  • Rule 6: Hydroxides usually do not dissolve. Group IA and Ba(OH)₂ are soluble exceptions; Ca(OH)₂ is slightly soluble.
  • Rule 7: CO₃²⁻, PO₄³⁻ and S²⁻ salts usually do not dissolve; Group IA and NH₄⁺ are exceptions.
